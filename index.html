BatteryMind Field Evidence

BatteryMind

Five findings from 16 interviews, each raised independently in at least three. The dots show how many of the 16.

  • 4 battery pack makers
  • 4 registered recyclers
  • 2 EPR consultants
  • 2 vehicle OEMs and dealers
  • 2 informal scrap aggregators
  • 2 experts

Filing is manageable. Getting the battery back is not.

10 of 16 interviews

“Filing is manageable. Collection. Definitely.”

Battery manufacturer, in-house compliance team

“The sale is the easy part. Nobody updates the record when the asset moves.”

Compliance lead, battery manufacturer

“Define traceability first. A serial number nobody owns the update for is a database with gaps.”

Battery circularity expert

What it changed: custody leads. Filing becomes the on-ramp.

Informal buyers win on rate, cash and speed, not on principle.

7 of 16 interviews

“Registration doesn’t automatically give you material.”

Registered recycler

“Rate important hai.” (Rate is what matters.)

Informal scrap aggregator

“We can run the process. The batteries do not arrive.”

Registered recycler

What it changed: formalising needs an incentive, not an argument.

Any extra step is dropped unless it saves time or earns money.

8 of 16 interviews

“If the app takes longer than calling my regular buyer, I will call my regular buyer.”

Informal scrap aggregator

“You can’t spend ten minutes entering details for a battery worth maybe few thousand rupees.”

Registered recycler

“QR is technology. Incentive is different.”

Independent industry expert

What it changed: the scan at return only happens if it pays, so we test the incentive before the technology.

A number nobody can check is not trusted.

7 of 16 interviews

“If you say 75%, why 75? What test? Which BMS parameters? What chemistry? How old?”

Battery manufacturer, in-house compliance team

“A clean digital entry is not verified material. It just looks authoritative.”

Registered recycler

“A serial number tells you identity. It does not tell you health.”

Service and dealer operations, EV network

What it changed: no health score from BMS data alone. A physical test and a published method come first.

Credits, not consultant fees, are the bigger compliance cost.

3 of 16 interviews

“The cost can be much more significant than my professional fee.”

EPR compliance consultant

What it changed: we re-sized the market from credit spend, and we price BatteryMind to replace an existing cost, never to add one.
